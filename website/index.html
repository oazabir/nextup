Features

Pick up where you left off.

▱

Keep the right task visible

The floating panel sits above your apps and shows what you’re working on and what’s next. Expand it to reorder priorities or mark a task done.

✦

Get a gentle check-in

By default, the panel flashes every five minutes. A brief title overlay can also remind you what you’re working on when you switch windows.

◷

See where your time went

Each task keeps its own elapsed time. Switch priorities with a shortcut and the previous timer pauses as the new one starts.

◇

Keep deadlines close

Add a due date and time to any task. The deadline appears below its title and becomes more prominent during the final three hours.

Keyboard shortcuts
